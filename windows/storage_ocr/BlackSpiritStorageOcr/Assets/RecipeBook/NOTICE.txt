Black Spirit Hub Recipe Book

Recipe facts, English item names and descriptions, and item icons were extracted from a legally installed Black Desert client for this non-commercial fan utility.
Cached item artwork is stored as lossless WebP at its native client dimensions; non-square client preview artwork is replaced with the bundled fallback.
The local screenshot scanner also bundles reduced 20x20 client icon tiles solely to distinguish usable Recipe Book materials from finished, event, equipment, box, coupon, and decorative items. Screenshot pixels never leave the device.
