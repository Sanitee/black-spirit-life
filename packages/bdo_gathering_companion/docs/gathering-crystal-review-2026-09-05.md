# Gathering crystals — September 5, 2026

The reference recommends up to 16 Crystal of Breathing Verdure in unlocked regular slots for Mastery/EXP. It does not select a preset or change the calculator's entered totals. Not every character has all 16 slots unlocked.

## Evidence

- [Official August 21, 2025 PC update](https://www.naeu.playblackdesert.com/en-US/News/Detail?groupContentNo=8972&countryType=en-US): each Crystal of Breathing Verdure gives Life Skill Mastery +15 and Life EXP +7%.
- [Official January 4, 2023 PC crystal-preset introduction (Spanish)](https://www.sa.playblackdesert.com/es-mx/News/Detail?countryType=es-mx&groupContentNo=2191): 12 default regular slots, a separate outfit-only slot, and two awakening-reform slots.
- [Current official Magic Crystals guide](https://www.naeu.playblackdesert.com/en-US/Wiki?wikiNo=319): the two Kkebiwater rewards from the Land of the Morning Light story unlock the bottom-left and bottom-right regular slots. The central slot accepts only Ancient Spirit crystals; accessory slots are exclusive, not extra regular slots.
- [Current official Garmoth guide](https://www.naeu.playblackdesert.com/en-US/Wiki?wikiNo=453): awakening reform grants two crystal slots. Thus the regular maximum is 12 + 2 + 2 = 16, not a promise that a character has unlocked all 16.
- [Current Vital Crystal game item description](https://bdocodex.com/us/item/15227/): +10 Life Skill Mastery. This is a replacement while upgrading, not an extra set to equip alongside 16 Verdure crystals. No fixed market savings are claimed.
- [Official September 3, 2026 PC update](https://www.naeu.playblackdesert.com/en-US/News/Detail?groupContentNo=10550&countryType=en-US): all JIN/BON/WON Dawn Crystal - Life EXP items were removed and replaced by Evergreen Orb/Shard. They are intentionally absent from this recommendation.


This is a Mastery/EXP choice, not a promise of best profit, weight, movement or combat stats. Bairog's calculator uses entered totals, not individual crystals.
