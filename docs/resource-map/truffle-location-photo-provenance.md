# Truffle locations and images

Reviewed August 28, 2026. This describes the dated sources, not a new PC live-game survey.

- 96 exact dots come from SomethingLovely's MIT dataset, historical through 2021.
- 13 named-area markers and four regional images come from Pearl Abyss's November 2, 2022 **console** notice. It listed 87 mushrooms and a 33% shorter respawn time.
- Regional images show general areas, not proof of an exact mushroom or cave entrance. They are attached only to the matching named area.

Area markers reuse reviewed node centres; the second Hasrah marker uses an existing historical coordinate. No offsets or routes are invented.

Community guides were reviewed, but no new exact point had enough reproducible evidence. Their screenshots are not bundled without reuse permission. Future additions need either a documented in-game survey or permission with author, URL, licence, caption and review date.

The image viewer supports keyboard navigation, Escape and up to 4× zoom. It shows the source and credit. An unmatched exact dot does not inherit a regional photo.
