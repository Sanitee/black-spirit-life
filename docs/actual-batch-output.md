# Record what you made

**Actually made** lets you enter the main item's real output when finishing an AFK batch.

- Leave it blank or enter 0 to use the estimate.
- Enter a positive whole number to use your result. Grouped numbers are accepted; negative values, fractions and numbers over 12 digits are rejected.
- Below 80% of the estimate, **Done** or **Next** asks you to confirm. Editing the amount clears that confirmation. Enter finishes editing only.

Ingredients are consumed for the crafts performed, not for the output entered. A shortfall stays queued; extra output goes into inventory. The change uses the normal completion and Undo flow. It does not change global yields or record actual byproducts.

The draft is not saved. Changing the recipe, goal, remaining amount, completed batch or capacity clears it, as do completion and Undo. Main and bonus results keep separate batch context.
