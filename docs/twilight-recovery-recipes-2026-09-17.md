# Twilight recovery — September 17, 2026

The [official update](https://blackdesert.pearlabyss.com/Asia/en-US/News/Notice/Detail?_boardNo=19820) added Heating recipes that recover **5 Twilight Crystals** from **1 matching Causality Shard** for necklaces, earrings, rings and belts.

Recipe Book keeps two recovery routes: 1 Apeiron → 10 Twilight Crystals, or 1 matching shard → 5. These are recovery recipes, not ordinary production. The Planner must not automatically consume valuable accessories or shards to make ingredients. Unknown EXP stays unknown.

The patch removed the old recipes for making base Apeiron accessories from Twilight Crystals. Their item identities and valid reform recipes remain. Saved data is not rewritten, and old user edits must not restore removed formulas.
