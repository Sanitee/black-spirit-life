# Black Spirit Life 0.1.5

> This is unofficial content which contains copyrighted materials and IP from
> Pearl Abyss, and is not official/endorsed content.

This update adds review-first screenshot entry for Inventory and expands the
Resource Map's planning tools.

## What's new

- Add inventory items from a searchable menu, edit amounts more quickly, and
  clear the saved inventory through an explicit confirmation flow.
- Paste or choose Black Desert inventory screenshots, review every recognized
  item and amount, and decide whether to replace or add to saved quantities.
  Recognition runs locally in an isolated Windows helper.
- See item-weight guidance and AFK load details while planning inventory.
- Use the Resource Map's new Gather and Workers menus to plan specific items,
  inspect worker networks, and compare lodging or storage-house investments.
- Keep substitute families and their effective ratios synchronized between the
  Craft Planner and Resource Map.
- Expanded reviewed acquisition guidance, processing formulas, and item-weight
  coverage for planner and inventory materials.
- Refined the Inventory layout, shell spacing, controls, and theme visuals
  across supported window sizes and text scales.

Updates from 0.1.4 download, install, and restart through the update bar inside
the app. Your planner data and settings stay in place. Stable and Beta profiles
remain separate.

Black Spirit Life is a free, noncommercial, and unofficial Black Desert fan
tool. Because the installer is not code-signed yet, Windows may show an
unknown-publisher or SmartScreen warning.
