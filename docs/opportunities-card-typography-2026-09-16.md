# Opportunities text sizes — September 16, 2026

Historical design notes. The [September 17 revision](opportunities-experience-sorting-2026-09-17.md) updated these sizes.

The September 16 design used 18 px names and main values, 13 px labels and 14 px supporting values. Profit was never larger than the recipe name. Crafting, Best Use and Exchanges shared `OpportunitiesVisualSpec`.

The redundant “Main item only” label was removed; the profit information button stayed. Card widths, colours, actions and calculations were unchanged.
